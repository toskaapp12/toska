writing prompts for acceptance

acceptance isn't a finish line, it's a tide. these prompts are for the days it comes in.

  • what are you pretending is fine right now
  • did you eat today. did you sleep. are you drinking water. be honest.
  • you got out of bed today. that's not nothing. say it like it counts.
  • what is the first thing that tasted good again. even a little.
  • name one small thing you did today that you didn't think you'd manage.
  • you're still here. say what got you through the worst hour.
  • what are you carrying that you could set down just for tonight.
  • did you go outside today. even to the door. that's allowed to count.
  • what felt almost normal today. don't rush past it.
  • you've been holding it together for everyone. who holds it for you.
  • what did you let yourself feel today instead of pushing it down.
  • name the smallest kindness you gave yourself this week.
  • what part of your day didn't hurt. start there.
  • you made it to right now. what helped, honestly.
  • what are you still pretending you don't miss.
  • did you laugh today. even once. it doesn't mean you're okay but it's something.
  • what would it look like to be gentle with yourself for the next ten minutes.
  • name one thing your body needs that you keep ignoring.
  • what's the first song you could listen to again without crying.
  • you cleaned one thing. you answered one text. what got done. be proud quietly.
  • what are you tired of pretending you're over.
  • did you rest today or just stop moving. there's a difference. be honest.
  • what small routine is keeping you upright right now.
  • name a moment today you forgot to be sad. let yourself have had it.
  • what's one thing you used to dread that felt okay today.
  • you survived the day you didn't think you'd survive. how does that sit.
  • what are you doing just to get through, and is it kind enough.
  • did anyone check on you today. did you let them in.
  • name the thing that felt like yours again. even for a second.
  • what hurts a little less than it did last week. notice it out loud.
  • you're allowed to not be fine and still be doing this right.
  • what did you make it through without checking their name. say it gently.
  • name one thing you can control tonight. just one. start small.
  • what are you white-knuckling that you could just let be hard.
  • did you drink water today. did you take the meds. did you breathe. be honest.
  • what's the first plan you made that didn't include them.
  • what did you let yourself enjoy today without guilt.
  • you woke up and chose to keep going. name what that took.
  • what part of you is starting to come back. don't scare it off. just notice.
  • name the bare minimum you did today and call it enough. because it is.
  • what are you pretending doesn't still ache on the quiet days.
  • did you eat something real today or just whatever was closest. no judgment.
  • what's one thing that felt steady when everything else didn't.
  • you got through dinner. through the night. through the morning. what helped.
  • name a small thing you looked forward to today. it doesn't have to be big.
  • what are you surviving right now that you won't always have to survive.
  • did you let yourself sit still today without filling the quiet. how was it.
  • what felt like the first easy breath in a while. hold onto that one.
