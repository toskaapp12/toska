writing prompts for anger

anger is grief with its jaw set. write it here instead of sending it — the point is the saying, not the delivery.

  • do you think they feel guilty or are you not even something to feel guilty about
  • what's the thing you can't forgive them for
  • are you angry or just really really sad. or both.
  • say it. the one thing you would've screamed if you weren't so busy being calm and reasonable
  • what's the lie they told that still makes your face hot when you remember it
  • you were so patient with them. who is gonna be patient with you
  • do you think they ever lie awake feeling sick about it or do they just sleep
  • name the thing you forgave that you shouldn't have
  • you're allowed to be furious. you don't have to be the bigger person tonight
  • what did they take that they didn't even want. they just didn't want you to have it
  • the apology you got was for the wrong thing wasn't it. what did they actually need to say
  • how many times did you make excuses for them out loud to people who were trying to warn you
  • what's the sentence of theirs you replay just to stay angry enough not to text back
  • do they get to keep the version of you that was soft. or are you taking her back
  • you gave them the benefit of the doubt every single time. tally it up now
  • what would you say to their face if you knew they'd actually have to sit there and hear it
  • they're out there telling their side. what's the part they're leaving out
  • how dare they be fine. say that. how dare they
  • what's the thing they did that you laughed off then that you want to throw across the room now
  • did they ever once choose you when it cost them something. or only when it was free
  • you're not crazy. you were never crazy. who made you doubt that and do you still let them
  • what promise of theirs do you want to read back to them word for word
  • they got to leave clean and you got the wreckage. where's the fairness in that
  • what's the comeback you thought of three days too late. say it here instead
  • did they make you small so they could feel big. how long did it work
  • what are you still defending them for and who are you protecting really
  • the worst part isn't that they did it. it's that they knew exactly what it would do to you and did it anyway. didn't they
  • you kept the receipts in your chest for months. dump them out
  • what did they call closure that was really just them needing you to be okay so they could feel less guilty
  • how many of your own needs did you shrink so they wouldn't feel crowded
  • they get to be the one who left. what story did that let them tell about you
  • what's the thing you bit back every time. let it out, no one here is grading your tone
  • did they ever say sorry or did they just say they felt bad that you were hurt
  • you weren't too much. you were exactly enough and they couldn't handle being seen by someone who was. say it meaner than that
  • what did loving them cost you that you only see the bill for now
  • they want to be friends. what's the laugh you held back when they asked
  • what's the favor you did at 2am that they never even thanked you for
  • you were loyal to someone who was auditioning replacements. how does that sit tonight
  • did they break it slow so they could pretend it wasn't them. catch them in it
  • what excuse of theirs do you want to set on fire right now
  • they cried too. but whose tears were performance and whose were the real wound
  • how long did you confuse their carelessness for something you'd done wrong
  • what's the version of events they'll tell their next person about you. correct the record here
  • you held the whole thing up alone and they called it a partnership. what's the word you actually want to use
  • did they ever fight for it or did they just let you do all the bleeding
  • what's the kindness you showed them that they used as a weapon later
  • they're sleeping fine tonight. that should make you angrier than it makes you sad. does it
  • what would younger you say if she saw how long you let them stay
  • say the thing you keep softening for everyone else. don't soften it here
