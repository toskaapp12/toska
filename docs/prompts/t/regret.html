writing prompts for regret

regret rereads everything. these prompts let you look back on purpose, once, instead of all night by accident.

  • what do you miss that has nothing to do with them as a person. like their dog. or their car. or their kitchen.
  • what's something small that still ruins you. a song. a street. a food.
  • what did they say that you still hear on repeat
  • what's the most pathetic thing you've done since it ended. no judgment here.
  • what's the last thing that made you cry about it. like actually cry.
  • what's the thing you wish you'd said before they walked out the door
  • there's a text you never sent. what's in it
  • what would you give to have one more ordinary tuesday with them
  • what's the apology you keep rehearsing for no one
  • finish this: i should never have
  • what did you pretend not to care about that you actually cared about so much
  • what's the last thing you lied to them about. and why
  • there's a version of you that fought harder. what would they have done
  • what do you reread late at night that you know you shouldn't
  • what did you take for granted right up until it was gone
  • finish this: if i'd only
  • what's the thing they asked you for that you didn't give them
  • what street do you go out of your way to avoid now
  • what's the smell that drops you straight back into their bed
  • what did you say in the last fight that you can't take back
  • there's a moment you keep going back to. which one. what would you change
  • what part of them do you still talk to when no ones around
  • what's the gift of theirs you still keep in a drawer
  • what did you stop doing for them that you wish you hadn't
  • finish this: i was too proud to
  • what's the order you can't place anymore because it was theirs too
  • what did you almost tell them and then didn't
  • what's the date on the calendar that still wrecks you
  • what would past you be furious at present you for
  • what's the song you put on just to feel it open back up
  • what did they warn you about that you ignored
  • what's the chore you used to hate that you miss doing with them
  • what did you assume you'd have more time for. and then you didn't

  • what do you still buy at the store out of habit for two

  • what did you let get cold and small instead of saying it out loud
  • what number do you still know by heart that you can't call
  • what did you do the day after that you still cringe about
  • what's the plan you two made that you can't unmake in your head
  • finish this: i keep thinking if i'd picked up the phone
  • what's the show you still can't watch the rest of without them
