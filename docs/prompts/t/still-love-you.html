writing prompts for still love you

you can love someone and not go back. these prompts hold both of those at once.

  • what would you say if they called right now. no thinking just say it.
  • say the thing you pretend you don't feel anymore
  • be honest. would you take them back right now if they asked.
  • do you still love them. you don't have to answer that. but do you.
  • if they walked back in tonight. no questions asked. would you let them.
  • be honest. how many of your thoughts still end with them.
  • you tell people you're over it. who are you actually trying to convince.
  • there's a version of you still waiting by the phone. say hi to her.
  • if love really left. why does their name still do that to your chest.
  • would you take the bad days back too. all of them. to have them again.
  • what part of them do you still defend in your head when no ones around.
  • you've forgiven them already haven't you. you just haven't told them.
  • if they called and only said your name. what would you do.
  • the feelings you say are gone. where do they go at 2am.
  • would you ruin all this peace just to feel them next to you once more.
  • be honest. do you still say goodnight to them in your head.
  • if they were happy without you. could you actually be glad. could you.
  • you stopped texting them. you didn't stop writing the texts though.
  • what would you give to be the one they think of first again.
  • if loving them was a mistake. why would you make it again. you would.
  • there's a sentence you've never said to them. say it here. just once.
  • would you still pick them. knowing exactly how it ends. knowing all of it.
  • be honest. is there anyone you compare to them. does anyone win.
  • if they asked for nothing. just to sit with you. would you say yes.
  • the love didn't leave when they did. what do you do with it now.
  • you keep their old messages. you're not gonna delete them. why.
  • if you saw them tomorrow. what would your hands want to do first.
  • would you tell them you still love them. if it changed nothing. would you.
  • be honest. when you say you miss them. do you mean you still want them.
  • there's a part of you that never agreed to the breakup. let it speak.
  • if they were standing outside right now. would you open the door.
  • you pretend it was just a season. but you still water it. don't you.
  • what would you say if they asked were you ever really over me.
  • would you wait. if they asked you to wait. how long. be honest.
  • the song comes on and you let it play the whole way through. why.
  • if i love you still lives in you. who is it waiting to reach.
  • be honest. do you want them back. or do you want who you were with them.
  • if they apologized. really apologized. would the love come rushing back.
  • you keep saying it was for the best. say what you actually feel.
  • would you trade being right for having them. tonight. yes or no.
  • there's a name your heart still calls home. you don't have to say it. but.
  • if they reached for your hand right now. would you pull away. would you.
  • be honest. how much of moving on is just acting until they can't tell.
  • the love you swore you killed. it's still breathing isn't it. quietly.
  • if they said i never stopped. what would you say back. don't think.
  • would you give up the closure just to keep the hope. some of you would.
  • you still set a place for them somehow. in your head. don't you.
  • if love is a choice. why does it keep choosing them without asking you.
  • be honest. if they came back changed and real. would you risk it all again.
  • the thing you most want them to know. you're not over it. write it anyway.
