Support

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Need help with toska, want to report a problem, or have a question? We're a small team and we read every message.

Contact us

Email salte@saltedevelopments.com. We aim to reply within a few business days. To help us help you, include your handle (Settings shows it), your device and iOS version, and a short description of what happened.

Reporting content or a user

You don't have to wait for us. Tap the “…” menu on any post, reply, or profile to report it, or to block a user so you never see their content again. There is zero tolerance for objectionable content on toska — we review every report and aim to act on violations within 24 hours.

About a removal

If we remove a post or reply of yours, you get an in-app notification saying what was removed, why, how it came to our attention (a report, our own review, or automated screening) and whether a person or an automated system decided. We never say who reported it. Think we got it wrong? Email salte@saltedevelopments.com with your handle and roughly when you posted, and a person will look again.

Content about you

If you don't use toska but believe a post describes or identifies you, email salte@saltedevelopments.com with the post's link or its text. You don't need an account. Our rules forbid naming or identifying private individuals, we aim to review within 24 hours, and we don't share your request with the post's author.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, posts, replies, and personal data.

Two things are kept after deletion, for safety and legal-defense purposes: reports (ones you filed and ones filed about your content, with the reported text) and our moderation records. They contain no more of your content than moderation required. Details are in the Privacy Policy, section 6.

If you're in crisis

toska is not a crisis service or a substitute for professional care. If you are in crisis, please contact the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988) or the Crisis Text Line (text HOME to 741741). If you are outside the US, please contact your local emergency services.
