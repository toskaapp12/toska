# toska — Community Guidelines

**Version 1.0 — July 17, 2026**

toska is for the things you couldn't say to them. Heavy feelings are welcome here. Hurting people is not. The short version:

**Say your thing. Don't name names. Don't hurt anyone. Don't try to find out who anyone is.**

## What's welcome

- Your grief, anger, longing, confusion, relief — raw and unpolished.
- Letters you'll never send. Whispers that vanish in an hour. Midnight thoughts.
- Replying to hold space for a stranger. "I've been there" is the most powerful thing on toska.

## The rules

1. **No names, no identifying details — theirs or anyone's.** This includes letters. A letter to "the person who left in March" is toska. A letter naming your ex, their workplace, or their Instagram is doxxing, and it comes down. Everyone here is anonymous — *including the people you're writing about*.
2. **Don't target real people.** No harassment, threats, or campaigns against anyone, on toska or off it.
3. **Never try to identify another user.** Guessing, outing, or investigating who someone is breaks the one promise this place is built on.
4. **Feeling your pain out loud is welcome. Instructions for harm are not.** You can say you're not okay — please do, and resources will be there. Content that instructs or encourages self-harm or suicide comes down.
5. **Nothing sexual, nothing involving minors.** Zero tolerance, immediate ban.
6. **No spam, ads, links, or bots.** This isn't a growth channel.
7. **One person, honestly.** No impersonation.

## How it works

Everything you post is screened automatically and may be held for human review. Every report gets reviewed — we aim for within 24 hours. Consequences scale from content removal, to a posting restriction, to a permanent ban. Zero-tolerance items (minors, sexual content, credible threats) skip the ladder.

**You have tools too:** report anything (long-press → report), and block anyone (they'll never know, and you disappear from each other's toska).

## If you're in crisis

toska is a place to feel, not a crisis service. If you're in danger of hurting yourself: **call or text 988** (US Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or **text HOME to 741741** (Crisis Text Line). The app will offer resources when it notices heavy language, but a hotline has humans, right now, for exactly this.
